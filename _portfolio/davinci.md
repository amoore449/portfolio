I built this drawing Automata at Harvard Makes 2020 inspired by the designs of Robert Sabuda, who in turn took inspiration from Leonardo Da Vinci’s “Mechanical Knight.” I have always loved automata, but I took an interest in this project specifically because of how it relates to early ideas of “computing.”

The wooden arm here is held by a spring (or in this case a rubber band) to the disk sitting in the center of the photo. As the disk is spun, the bumps and ridges of the disk are translated to the movement of the arm. In theory, this makes the arm “programmable” to draw the same design over and over based on the shape of the disk. 

I used a drill press, scroll saw, and laser cutter for this project.
