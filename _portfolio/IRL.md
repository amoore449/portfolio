Conference paper published in IROS 2022 on human-in-the-loop reinforcement learning within a continuous environment. This paper expands in discussion of CAIR, the first IntRL algorithm for continuous action spaces.CAIR blends environment- and teacher-learned policies by weighting them based on their agreement, enabling robust learning even with noisy human feedback
