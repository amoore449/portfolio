During the Summer of 2026, I worked as the Visual Arts Teaching Artist at Mr. D's Music Club. There, I designed weekly sets for youth play production and led art classes with Campers aged 7 to 12 to construct the designs. In addition to showing their work as part of the set onstage, each camper took home a finished large scale art piece they had built themselves.
